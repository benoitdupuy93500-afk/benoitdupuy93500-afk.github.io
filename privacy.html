Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Faceless AI Publisher ("the App") is a private tool operated by an independent creator to manage their own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the App accesses

  • Upload and update videos, thumbnails and video metadata on the authorized channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Read the authorized channel's basic information and performance statistics such as views, click-through rate and watch time (YouTube Analytics API).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the creator's own videos and to measure their performance. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the operator's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept as long as the channel is operated. Access can be revoked at any time from Google security settings; stored tokens are then deleted. Deletion requests can be sent to the contact address below.
